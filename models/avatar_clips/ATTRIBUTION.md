# AI4Bharat INCLUDE Dataset Attribution

This directory contains retrieved avatar video clips sourced from the **AI4Bharat INCLUDE** dataset for sign realization in ClarifySign.

## Attribution Details

- **Dataset:** INCLUDE: A Large Scale Dataset for Indian Sign Language Recognition
- **Authors:** Prem Selvaraj, Gokul N.C., Pratyush Kumar, Mitesh M. Khapra
- **Institution:** AI4Bharat, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Madras
- **Publication:** ACM Multimedia 2020 (https://doi.org/10.1145/3394171.3413528)
- **Source Record:** Zenodo record 4010759 (https://zenodo.org/records/4010759)
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (**CC BY 4.0**) (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- **Signers:** Recorded with deaf students and instructors from St. Louis School for the Deaf, Adyar, Chennai, India.

## Terms of Use & Redistribution

Per the terms of CC BY 4.0:
1. **Attribution:** Appropriate credit is given above with links to the original publication, creators, and license.
2. **Usage in ClarifySign:** Clips are used as an isolated sign retrieval bank for shopkeeper domain concepts. Signs without clean, approved clips display an explicit "not yet available" state; no synthetic or unverified poses are substituted.
